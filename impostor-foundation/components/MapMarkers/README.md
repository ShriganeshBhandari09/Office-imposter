# Map markers
The pieces drawn on top of the office floor plan.

- **Door**: an 8px gap in the wall filled with `map-floor`, a 2px `map-door` jamb at each end, a faint centre line, and a 6px `map-door` glow at 25%.
- **Glass wall**: 6px `map-glass`; exterior glass alternates 36px glass and 4px `map-wall-outer` mullions.
- **Vent**: a 20 × 14 `#0C0F13` grille with 1px slats every 3px, radius 3. Its pair label (V1–V4) appears only on the map screen.
- **Task tag**: a 13px `map-task` pill with the task number, top-right of the object. Map screen only.
- **Emergency button**: a 24px dark table, a 13px yellow-and-black caution ring, and a 6.5px red button with `map-emergency` glow, ringed by 8 chairs.
